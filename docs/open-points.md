# Open points

The one list of open work. Every other document says what exists and what the host tests prove; where it says "not verified", the item is here with the person who has to act. Nothing is listed here that a later document describes as done: when an item is finished, it is removed in the commit that finishes it.

The owner is the person who owns the device and the repository. Software work that needs no device is not listed: it is done before it is merged.

| 25 | Power save, and why `modem_sleep` is off | Your `settings.toml` has `modem_sleep = false`, so the device never sleeps and runs at about 130 mA (about eleven hours). | 26 | The charge current on the device | Built and host-tested ([D-14](decisions.md)): the firmware limits the charge current to 960 mA and switches the charger's watchdog off at every start. | 20 | Operations that block the main loop | The motion page blocks the screen for about 11 seconds while the sensor starts; the e-ink wait and the card's safe write can block too, and none of it is measured against the loop.
